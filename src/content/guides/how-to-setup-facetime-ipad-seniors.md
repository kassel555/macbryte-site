## What You Need Before Starting

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| **iPad** | Any model with a front camera (iPad 5th gen+, iPad Air 2+, iPad mini 4+, any iPad Pro) |
| **Apple ID** | Your Apple account email & password. Don't have one? We'll create it in Step 1. |
| **Wi-Fi** | FaceTime works over Wi-Fi only -- no cellular plan needed on iPad |

---

## Step 1: Create or Sign In with Your Apple ID

*Your Apple ID is the key to all Apple services. If you already have one, skip to Step 2.*

1. Open **Settings** (gear icon on Home Screen)
2. Tap **"Sign in to your iPad"** at the top of the left sidebar
3. **If you HAVE an Apple ID:** Enter your email and password -> tap **Next**
4. **If you DON'T have an Apple ID:** Tap *"Don't have an Apple ID?"* -> *"Create Apple ID"* -> follow prompts
   - Use your personal email address
   - Create a memorable password -- **write it down!**
   - Answer security questions (choose answers you'll remember)
5. Complete two-factor authentication if prompted (code sent to your phone/email)

> **Pro tip:** Use the same Apple ID on all your Apple devices (iPhone, Mac, iPad) -- contacts, photos, and FaceTime sync automatically.

---

## Step 2: Turn On FaceTime

1. In **Settings**, scroll down the left sidebar and tap **FaceTime** (green video camera icon)
2. Toggle the **FaceTime** switch to **ON** (green)
3. Under *"You Can Be Reached By FaceTime At"* -- ensure your Apple ID email has a checkmark
4. **Optional:** Add your phone number if you also have an iPhone with the same Apple ID
5. Toggle **FaceTime Live Photos** ON if you want to capture photos during calls

---

## Step 3: Add Family & Friends to Contacts

*FaceTime uses your Contacts app. Adding people with photos makes calling as easy as tapping a face.*

1. Open **Contacts** app (silhouette icon)
2. Tap the **+** button (top-right)
3. Enter:
   - First Name, Last Name
   - **Phone Number** (for iPhone users)
   - **Email Address** (Apple ID email for iPad/Mac users) -- *this is what FaceTime uses!*
4. Tap **"Add Photo"** -> Choose Photo or Take Photo -> select a clear picture
5. Tap **Done** (top-right)
6. Repeat for each person you want to FaceTime (start with 3-5 closest contacts)

> **Why photos matter:** When a call comes in, you'll see their face -- not just a name. Much easier to recognize!

---

## Step 4: Make Your First FaceTime Call

*Two ways to call -- both work. Try Method A first (easiest).*

### Method A -- From Contacts (Recommended)
1. Open **Contacts** -> tap the person's name
2. Tap the **green video camera icon** under their name/photo

### Method B -- From FaceTime App
1. Open **FaceTime** -> tap **"New FaceTime"**
2. Type the name -> tap **video camera icon**

### During the Call
- Tap the screen to show controls: flip camera, mute, end call (red X), speaker
- To end: tap the **red X button**

---

## Step 5: Receive a FaceTime Call

| Situation | What to Do |
|-----------|------------|
| iPad **unlocked** | Tap green **Accept** (video) or **Accept** (audio only) |
| iPad **locked** | Swipe **right** on the green video slider |
| **Missed call** | Notification appears -> tap to call back |

---

## Step 6: Helpful Settings for Seniors

*Adjust these once for a better experience.*

| Setting | Path | Why It Helps |
|---------|------|--------------|
| **Larger text** | Settings -> Display & Brightness -> Text Size -> drag right | Everything easier to read |
| **Speaker on by default** | Settings -> FaceTime -> toggle **Speaker** ON | Hands-free, louder audio |
| **Block spam callers** | Settings -> FaceTime -> **Blocked Contacts** -> add numbers | Stops known spam |
| **Silence unknown callers** | Settings -> FaceTime -> toggle **Silence Unknown Callers** ON | Non-contacts go straight to voicemail -- *highly recommended!* |

## Troubleshooting Quick Fixes

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| "FaceTime unavailable" | Check Wi-Fi -> Settings -> FaceTime -> ensure toggled ON |
| Can't hear/be heard | Tap microphone icon (mute) -> tap again | Check iPad volume buttons |
| "No contacts show in FaceTime" | Contacts must have **email address** (not just phone) for iPad-to-iPad |
| Video freezes | Weak Wi-Fi -> move closer to router, or switch to audio-only |
| Forgot Apple ID password | iforgot.apple.com -> reset via email or security questions |
