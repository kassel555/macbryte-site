## What Is Phishing?

**Phishing** (pronounced "fishing") is when scammers send fake emails, texts, or calls pretending to be from:
- Your bank (RBC, TD, Scotiabank, etc.)
- Government (CRA, Service Canada, Police)
- Delivery companies (Canada Post, Purolator, UPS)
- Big brands (Amazon, Netflix, Apple, Microsoft)
- **Even your own family** ("Grandma, it's me...")

Their goal: **get you to click a link, call a number, or give up passwords, SIN, credit card, gift cards, or crypto.**

> **Why seniors?** Scammers assume we're more trusting, less tech-savvy, and have savings. **Knowledge beats them every time.**

---

## The 5 Universal Red Flags

*Memorize these -- they appear in 95% of phishing attempts.*

| Red Flag | What It Looks Like | Why It Works |
|----------|-------------------|--------------|
| **1. URGENCY / THREAT** | "Immediate action required!" "Account closes in 24 hours!" "Police coming now!" | Panic bypasses thinking |
| **2. GENERIC GREETING** | "Dear Customer," "Hello," "Hi Friend" -- **not your name** | Legit companies know your name |
| **3. SENDER MISMATCH** | Says "RBC" but email is `security@rbc-alerts.xyz` -- **hover to see real address** | Looks familiar at first glance |
| **4. LINK MISMATCH** | Text says `td.com` but hover shows `td-login.phishersite.com` | You trust the visible text |
| **5. ASKS FOR SECRETS** | Password, PIN, SIN, credit card, **gift cards, crypto** | **LEGITIMATE COMPANIES NEVER ASK** |

> **Memory aid**: **UGLY ASK** = **U**rgency, **G**eneric greeting, **L**ink mismatch, **Y** (sender mismatch), **A**sks for secrets, **S** (you spot it), **K**nowledge wins.

## Real Phishing Examples (Safe to Study)

*Redacted but realistic -- these target Canadian seniors daily.*

### Email: "CRA Tax Refund"
> **From:** `refunds@cra-gov-ca.net` (NOT `@cra-arc.gc.ca`)  
> **Subject:** Interac e-Transfer: $1,247.32 Tax Refund  
> **Body:** "You have an unclaimed refund. Click to deposit: `cra-refund-deposit.xyz`"  
> **Reality:** CRA **never** emails refund links. They mail cheques or direct deposit.

### Text: "Interac e-Transfer"
> **From:** `778-555-0199` (random 10-digit, not 5-digit short code)  
> **Body:** "Interac: $500 from John Smith. Accept: `interac-accept.biz`"  
> **Reality:** Real Interac texts come from **5-digit short codes** (e.g., 100001), never links.

### Call: "Police / CRA Threat"
> **Caller ID:** "Ottawa Police" (spoofed)  
> **Script:** "Officer Miller. Your SIN used for fraud. Buy $2,000 Google Play cards. Read codes. Don't tell anyone."  
> **Reality:** **Police/CRA never demand gift cards/crypto. Ever.** Hang up.

### Email: "Amazon Order Cancelled"
> **From:** `orders@amazon-security.net` (NOT `@amazon.ca`)  
> **Body:** "Order #738291 cancelled. Verify: `amazon-verify.biz`"  
> **Reality:** Check orders in **Amazon app directly**. Don't click.

### Text: "Grandparent Scam" (#1 Senior Scam)
> **From:** Unknown number  
> **Body:** "Grandma, it's me. Phone broke. In trouble. Need $500 e-transfer NOW. Don't tell Mom."  
> **Reality:** **CALL YOUR GRANDCHILD DIRECTLY.** Scammers use social media to find names.

## The 3-Second Rule

*When a message triggers urgency, fear, or excitement -- **PAUSE. Count to 3.** Ask:*

1. **Was I expecting this?**
2. **Does it have red flags?** (Urgency? Generic greeting? Weird sender/link?)
3. **Can I verify independently?** (Call number on card, log into app directly, call family)

> **If ANY answer is "No" or "I don't know" -> DON'T CLICK. DON'T CALL. DON'T REPLY.**

---

## Verify Independently -- The Golden Rule

> **NEVER use contact info IN the suspicious message. Always go to the source.**

| If Message Claims To Be... | Verify By... |
|----------------------------|--------------|
| **Your Bank** | Call number on **BACK of your debit/credit card** |
| **CRA / Government** | Call **1-800-959-8281** (CRA) -- never trust caller ID |
| **Delivery (Canada Post, etc.)** | Track in **official app** or website directly |
| **Family Member** | Call them on their **KNOWN number** |
| **Company (Amazon, Netflix, etc.)** | Google **"Company name contact"** -- don't click email links |

## Report & Delete -- Protect Others

| Channel | How to Report |
|---------|---------------|
| **Email** | Gmail: "Report phishing" / Outlook: Junk -> Phishing / Yahoo: Spam -> Report Phishing |
| **Text** | Forward to **7726 (SPAM)** -- free, alerts your carrier |
| **Call** | Hang up. Block number. Report: **Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre 1-888-495-8501** |
| **If you clicked/gave info** | **Call bank IMMEDIATELY** (number on card) -> Change passwords -> Enable 2FA -> Monitor credit |

## Troubleshooting: "But What If...?"

| Situation | Do This |
|-----------|---------|
| "I already clicked the link" | Close browser. Run antivirus scan. Change passwords on another device. Enable 2FA. |
| "I gave them my password" | Change it **immediately** on that site + anywhere you reused it. Enable 2FA. |
| "I bought gift cards / sent crypto" | **Stop.** No more payments. Call police (non-emergency). Report to Anti-Fraud Centre. |
| "They have my SIN" | Contact Service Canada 1-800-206-7218. Place fraud alert with Equifax/TransUnion. Freeze credit. |
| "They remote-accessed my computer" | Disconnect internet. Take to trusted tech for malware scan. Change ALL passwords from clean device. |
