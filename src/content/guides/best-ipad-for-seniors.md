## Why iPad mini (A17 Pro) Wins for Most Seniors

### Physical Advantages
- **293 grams** = weight of a paperback book -> hold one-handed for hours
- **8.3"** -> fits in purse, coat pocket, kitchen stand
- **No Home button** -> full-screen, Face ID (works with masks/glasses)
- **Landscape front camera** -> natural FaceTime in stand/case

### Screen Quality
- **Liquid Retina** (2266x1488, 326 ppi) -- sharp, bright, true-to-life colors
- **Wide color (P3)** -- photos look amazing
- **True Tone** -- adjusts warmth to room light (easier on eyes)
- **Anti-reflective coating** -- less glare than base iPad

### Performance That Lasts
- **A17 Pro chip** (same as iPhone 15 Pro) -> 5+ years iOS updates
- **Apple Intelligence ready** -- AI features coming (summaries, writing tools, smarter Siri)
- **8 GB RAM** -> multitasking smooth (Split View, Slide Over)

### Accessibility Ready
- All iOS accessibility features (Zoom, VoiceOver, Switch Control, etc.)
- **Apple Pencil (USB-C)** support -- write, mark up, sign documents
- **Smart Folio** case -- stand + cover, no Bluetooth pairing

## Why iPad 10th Gen (2022) Is the Smart Budget Choice

### $349 -- Unbeatable Value
- Does **everything** a senior needs: FaceTime, email, photos, web, banking, books
- **10.9" screen** -- larger text, easier to tap
- **USB-C** -- universal charging, works with any USB-C hub/accessory
- **Landscape front camera** -- fixed the weird side-camera issue

### Compromises
- **477 g** -- 63% heavier than mini (two hands for long use)
- **A14 Bionic** (2020 chip) -- 3-4 years updates left
- **60 Hz screen** (not 120 Hz ProMotion) -- fine for reading, not buttery smooth
- **No anti-reflective coating** -- more glare in bright rooms
- **Apple Pencil (1st gen) only** -- needs USB-C adapter, charges awkwardly

> **Verdict:** If budget is tight -> **this is the one.** Upgrade to 128 GB ($449) -- 64 GB fills fast with photos.

## Why to Skip iPad Air & Pro (For Seniors)

| Model | Why It's Overkill |
|-------|-------------------|
| **iPad Air (M2)** | M2 chip = laptop-class. You'll never use 10% of it. $170 more than mini for same size (11"). |
| **iPad Pro (M4)** | Ultra Retina XDR (OLED) = stunning but $670 more. M4 = desktop-class. Keyboard + Pencil = $500+. Total = laptop money. |

> **Exception:** If you **replace a laptop** -- write a memoir, manage complex spreadsheets, edit video for family -- Air M2 + Magic Keyboard = reasonable laptop alternative.

## Storage: How Much Do You Need?

| Usage | Minimum | Recommended |
|-------|---------|-------------|
| **Email, FaceTime, web, few apps** | 64 GB | **128 GB** |
| **Photos (1,000+), some apps, offline Netflix** | 128 GB | **256 GB** |
| **Lots of photos/videos, many apps, offline media** | 256 GB | **512 GB** |

> **My advice:** **Buy 128 GB minimum.** 64 GB = "Storage Full" in 6 months. iCloud Photos helps but local cache + apps + iOS updates eat space. $150 more now saves headache later.

## Cellular vs Wi-Fi Only

| Option | Cost | Best For |
|--------|------|----------|
| **Wi-Fi Only** | Base price | Home Wi-Fi, coffee shops, library, family's house |
| **Cellular** | +$150-250 + monthly plan | **No home internet**, frequent travel, cottage, backup internet |

> **Senior tip:** **Wi-Fi only + phone hotspot.** Your iPhone shares data -> iPad connects free. No extra monthly bill. Enable: iPhone Settings -> Personal Hotspot -> ON.

## What About Refurbished / Used / Older Models?

| Option | Verdict | Why |
|--------|---------|-----|
| **Apple Certified Refurbished** | **OK** | 1-year warranty, new battery, new shell, 15% off. Check `apple.ca/shop/refurbished` |
| **Used (Kijiji/FB Marketplace)** | **AVOID** | No warranty, unknown battery, possible iCloud lock, screen damage |
| **iPad 9th Gen (2021)** | **AVOID** | Lightning port, old design, side camera, 3 GB RAM, 2-3 years updates left |
| **iPad 8th Gen (2020) or older** | **HARD NO** | No iOS 18+, security risk, slow, Lightning, 32 GB base |

> **Rule:** If it doesn't run **iOS 18** (released Sept 2024), **don't buy it.** Security updates stop -> banking apps break -> vulnerability.
