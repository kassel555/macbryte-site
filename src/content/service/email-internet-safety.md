## What You'll Learn (Customized to Your Goals)

### Email Mastery -- So You Stay Connected Without the Confusion
- **Composing**: New message, reply, reply all, forward -- when to use each
- **Attachments**: Adding photos, PDFs, documents -- size limits, multiple files
- **Organization**: Folders/labels (Family, Medical, Bills, Travel), archiving vs deleting
- **Contacts**: Adding, editing, groups (e.g., "Family" for one-click emailing)
- **Signatures**: Auto-add your name, phone, "Sent from my iPad"
- **Unsubscribe**: Cleaning up newsletters you never read

### Spam & Phishing Defense -- So Your Inbox Stays Safe
- **Spam folder**: What goes there, when to check, never click links in spam
- **Phishing red flags**:
  - Urgent/threatening language ("Account closed in 24 hours!")
  - Generic greetings ("Dear Customer" vs your name)
  - Suspicious sender (address doesn't match company)
  - Weird links (hover to see real destination -- don't click!)
  - Requests for passwords, SIN, banking info -- **legitimate companies never ask**
- **Reporting**: Mark as spam/phishing -- helps everyone
- **Two-factor authentication (2FA)**: Turn it on for email -- the #1 security upgrade

### Safe Web Browsing -- So You Find Answers, Not Problems
- **Address bar vs search bar** -- type `td.com` directly, don't search "TD Bank login"
- **HTTPS & the lock icon** -- never enter info on `http://` sites
- **Bookmarks** for banks, government, pharmacy -- avoid typosquatting (`tdcannada.com` != `td.com`)
- **Pop-ups**: Close with X (not "OK"), enable blocker in browser settings
- **Downloads**: Only from trusted sites, scan with Windows Defender/macOS before opening

### Search Skills -- So Google Works for You
- **Phrases in quotes**: `"Ottawa senior transit"` finds exact phrase
- **Site search**: `site:ottawa.ca garbage schedule` searches only that site
- **Exclude words**: `apple -fruit` finds tech, not food
- **Evaluating results**: Skip ads (labeled "Sponsored"), prefer .gov/.edu/.ca, check dates

### Password Management -- So You're Secure Without Memorizing 50 Passwords
- **Unique password per site** -- reuse = one breach compromises everything
- **Passphrases**: `Correct-Horse-Battery-Staple-2024!` > `Password123`
- **Password manager** (Bitwarden, 1Password, Apple Passwords) -- one master password
- **2FA everywhere**: Authenticator app (Microsoft/Google Authenticator) > SMS codes
- **Emergency access**: Share master password location with trusted family member

### Online Forms & Government Services -- So You Handle Business From Home
- **My ServiceOntario**, **CRA My Account**, **Ottawa Public Library** -- creating accounts
- **Digital ID**: Verify identity online (video call with agent)
- **Filling PDFs**: Save, sign (Preview on Mac, Edge on Windows), email back
- **Accessibility**: Screen reader friendly, zoom, high contrast modes

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What email providers do you support?**  
Gmail, Outlook/Hotmail, Yahoo, iCloud, Rogers/Bell/Telus, custom domains -- all work the same way.

**Do I need a smartphone for 2FA?**  
Yes, for authenticator apps. If you don't have one, we can use SMS codes (less secure but better than nothing) or a physical security key (YubiKey).

**Can you help me recover a hacked account?**  
I can guide you through the recovery process (password reset, 2FA re-enable, check forwarding rules). For active breaches, I'll refer you to specialized help.

**What if I get a scary phone call saying "Microsoft detected a virus"?**  
Hang up. Microsoft/Apple never call unsolicited. I'll teach you the "official number callback" rule: look up the real number, call back.

**Do you teach about social media privacy (Facebook, etc.)?**  
Yes -- profile visibility, friend requests from strangers, scam messages, 2FA on social accounts.

**How many sessions for email + safety?**  
3-4 sessions covers the essentials. Password manager setup = 1 session. Phishing practice = 1 session. Ongoing "is this legit?" support = included.
