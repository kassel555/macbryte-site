## What You'll Learn (Customized to Your Goals)

### Online Banking Setup -- So You're In Control From Day One
- **First login**: Registration, temporary password, creating a strong permanent one
- **Two-factor authentication (2FA)**: Authenticator app (best) or SMS -- **mandatory for banking**
- **Security questions**: Choose answers only you know (lie consistently -- "mother's maiden name" = "PurpleTulip73")
- **Biometric login**: Face ID / Touch ID / Windows Hello -- convenient AND secure
- **Trusted devices**: Register your home computer/phone -- new device = extra verification

### Daily Banking Tasks -- So Routine Becomes Effortless
- **Check balances & transactions** -- spot unfamiliar charges instantly
- **E-transfers (Interac)**: Send/receive -- auto-deposit setup, security question tips
- **Bill payees**: Add once (hydro, credit card, phone, tax) -> pay in 3 clicks
- **Scheduled/recurring payments**: "Set and forget" for rent, utilities, subscriptions
- **Download statements**: PDF for records, tax time, proof of address
- **Message centre**: Secure bank messages -- never email sensitive info

### Credit Cards & Digital Wallets -- So You're Protected on Every Purchase
- **Lock/unlock card instantly** in app (lost card? freeze in seconds)
- **Transaction alerts**: Push notification for every charge >$0 -- real-time fraud detection
- **Virtual card numbers**: For online shopping (Apple Pay, Google Pay, bank-specific)
- **Apple Pay / Google Pay / Samsung Pay**: Tokenized -- merchant never sees real card number
- **Credit score monitoring**: Free in most bank apps -- check monthly

### Online Shopping Safety -- So You Get What You Ordered, Not Scammed
- **Trusted retailers only**: Known brands, `.ca` for Canadian, check reviews on independent sites
- **HTTPS + lock icon** -- never checkout on `http://`
- **Payment hierarchy**: Credit card > PayPal > Debit > E-transfer > Wire/crypto (never wire/crypto)
- **Guest checkout** -- don't create accounts on one-off sites
- **Save receipts**: Screenshot + email to self / folder in email
- **Return policies**: Check before buying -- restocking fees, return shipping, time limits

### Scam Recognition -- So You Spot the Tricks Targeting Seniors
| Scam Type | Red Flags | Your Defense |
|-----------|-----------|--------------|
| **Grandparent / Emergency** | "Grandma, I'm in jail/hospital -- need money NOW, don't tell parents!" | **Hang up. Call grandchild directly.** |
| **CRA / Police / Government** | "You owe back taxes -- pay by gift card/bitcoin or we arrest you" | **Government never demands gift cards/crypto. Hang up.** |
| **Tech Support** | "Microsoft detected a virus -- give me remote access" | **Microsoft never calls. Hang up.** |
| **Romance / Catfish** | Online relationship -> sudden crisis -> "send money for visa/flight" | **Never send money to someone you haven't met in person.** |
| **Phishing Email/Text** | "Your package delayed -- click here" / "Bank account locked -- verify now" | **Don't click. Go to site directly. Report.** |
| **Fake Invoice/Refund** | "You overpaid -- click to claim refund" / "Invoice attached" | **Verify with company directly. Don't open unexpected attachments.** |

### Advanced Protection -- So You're Ahead of the Curve
- **Credit freeze** (Equifax/TransUnion) -- free, prevents new accounts in your name
- **Password manager** (Bitwarden, 1Password, Apple Passwords) -- unique 20-char passwords everywhere
- **Email aliases** (SimpleLogin, Firefox Relay) -- unique email per site = breach isolation
- **Privacy.com virtual cards** -- set spending limits, pause/delete per merchant
- **Regular credit report review** -- annualcreditreport.com (US) / Equifax/TransUnion free (CA)

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is it safe to do banking on my iPad/phone?**  
Yes -- bank apps use bank-grade encryption. Safer than web browser if you: keep OS updated, use biometric login, enable auto-lock, don't jailbreak/root.

**What if I don't have a smartphone for 2FA?**  
Most banks offer: SMS codes (less secure but OK), hardware token (little keyfob), or voice call. We'll set up what your bank supports.

**Can you help me if I've already been scammed?**  
I can guide immediate steps: call bank (freeze cards), report to Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre (1-888-495-8501), change passwords, check credit report. For recovery, I'll refer to specialists.

**Do you teach about cryptocurrency / Bitcoin?**  
Only to say: **legitimate organizations never demand payment in crypto.** If someone asks for Bitcoin/gift cards/wire -- it's a scam. 100% of the time.

**How many sessions for banking + shopping safety?**  
3-4 sessions covers essentials + password manager. Scam drill practice = included. Ongoing "is this legit?" = included forever.

**What if my bank doesn't have an app?**  
All Big 5 (RBC, TD, Scotiabank, BMO, CIBC) + most credit unions have apps. If yours truly doesn't, we'll secure the web login: 2FA, bookmarks, password manager.
