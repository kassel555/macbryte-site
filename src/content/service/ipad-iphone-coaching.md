## What You'll Learn (Customized to Your Goals)

### Getting Started — So You Feel Comfortable Holding Your Device
- Turning on/off, charging, volume buttons, home screen layout
- Making text larger, turning on voice control, adjusting brightness
- Understanding the difference between Wi-Fi and cellular (and why it matters)

### Staying Connected — So You Can See Your Grandkids Every Sunday
- Phone calls, Contacts, Messages (SMS vs iMessage — what's the difference?)
- **FaceTime: video and audio calls — one tap to connect**
- Sharing photos directly in Messages

### Photos & Memories — So 40 Years of Family Pictures Stay Organized
- Taking photos and videos, finding them in the Photos app
- Creating Albums (e.g., "Grandkids 2024," "Family Reunion July")
- Sharing via AirDrop, Message, Email — and understanding iCloud Photos basics
- Deleting duplicates and blurry shots without fear

### Essential Apps — So Your iPad Becomes Useful, Not Intimidating
- **Mail**: Send/receive emails with attachments (photos, documents)
- **Safari**: Search the web, save bookmarks, avoid pop-ups
- **Calendar & Reminders**: Appointments, medication times, birthdays
- **App Store**: Download/update apps safely (WhatsApp, Zoom, library apps)
- **Weather, Maps, Notes**: Daily helpers

### Safety & Settings — So You're Protected Without Being Paranoid
- Passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID — which to use
- **Find My iPad** — locate a lost device instantly
- Emergency SOS and Medical ID setup
- **Silence Unknown Callers** — stop spam calls cold
- Software updates: why they matter, how to do them safely
- iCloud Backup — "set it and forget it" peace of mind

### Optional Extras (Add When You're Ready)
- WhatsApp/Zoom for family group calls
- Apple Pay for contactless payments
- Podcasts, Books/Audible for entertainment
- Health app: medications, steps, emergency contacts
- Smart home (HomeKit) — lights, thermostat, doorbell
