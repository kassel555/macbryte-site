## What You'll Learn (Customized to Your Goals)

### Microsoft Word -- So Your Documents Look Professional
- **Letters & formatting**: Fonts, spacing, alignment, bullet/numbered lists
- **Templates**: Resumes, letters, fax covers, meeting agendas -- don't start from scratch
- **Insert**: Photos, tables, page numbers, headers/footers, hyperlinks
- **Track Changes & Comments**: Collaborate with family on shared documents
- **Save/Export**: .docx (editable), .pdf (locked, universal), print to PDF
- **Accessibility**: Alt text for images, heading styles for screen readers

### Microsoft Excel -- So Your Numbers Make Sense
- **Spreadsheet basics**: Cells, rows, columns, sheets, naming tabs
- **Budgets & lists**: Monthly expenses, medication tracker, inventory, gift lists
- **Formulas that matter**:
  - `=SUM(A1:A10)` -- total a column
  - `=AVERAGE(B1:B12)` -- average
  - `=TODAY()` -- auto-updating date
  - Simple `IF` -- "If over budget, show RED"
- **Formatting**: Currency, percentages, dates, conditional formatting (highlight overspending)
- **Charts**: Pie (expense breakdown), Bar (monthly comparison), Line (trends)
- **Print setup**: Fit to page, repeat headers, print area

### PowerPoint -- So Your Stories Shine
- **Slide basics**: Title, content, layout choices (Title Only, Two Content, Picture with Caption)
- **Photos + captions**: Family reunions, travel albums, milestone celebrations
- **Text rules**: 6 words/line, 6 lines/slide -- less is more
- **Transitions & animations**: Subtle (Fade) -- avoid dizzying effects
- **Presenter View**: See notes on your screen, audience sees slides only
- **Export**: .pptx, .pdf handouts, video (File -> Export -> Create Video)

### Google Workspace (Free Alternative) -- So You Can Collaborate Anywhere
- **Docs** = Word online, **Sheets** = Excel online, **Slides** = PowerPoint online
- **Auto-save** to Google Drive -- never lose work
- **Real-time collaboration**: Edit together with family (grandkids love this!)
- **Access anywhere**: Phone, tablet, library computer -- just sign in
- **Compatibility**: Open/edit Word/Excel/PPT files, download as Office formats

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need to buy Microsoft Office?**  
No. If you have Windows, you may have free Office Online. Google Workspace is 100% free with a Google account. I'll help you decide.

**What if I have a Mac?**  
Pages, Numbers, Keynote (Apple's free apps) work similarly. I teach those too -- or Microsoft 365 for Mac / Google Workspace.

**Can you help with a specific project I have in mind?**  
Absolutely. Bring your idea -- memoir, family tree, club treasurer report, recipe book -- we'll build it together.

**How many sessions for a project?**  
Simple doc (letter, list) = 1 session. Budget spreadsheet = 2-3. Slideshow with 30+ photos = 2-3. Memoir = ongoing.

**Do you teach advanced features (pivot tables, mail merge)?**  
Only if you need them. Most seniors don't. I focus on practical daily skills.

**Can we use my existing files?**  
Yes! Bring your half-finished spreadsheet, messy document, old slideshow -- we'll clean it up and make it work.
