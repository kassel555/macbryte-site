Common iPad Problems We Help With

Many people feel embarrassed asking for iPad help because they think they should already know how it works. You do not need to feel that way. Technology changes quickly, and small changes in settings, passwords, apps, or updates can make even simple tasks confusing.

We can help you slow things down and make sense of it. You can learn how to make video calls with family, send photos, read and reply to emails, use Safari, manage apps, change text size, silence annoying notifications, or understand what those pop-ups are asking you to do.

iPad Help Questions

Do you teach absolute beginners how to use an iPad?

Yes. Macbryte works with seniors and beginners who may be using an iPad for the first time. Lessons start with the basics and move at your pace.

Can you help with FaceTime, email, and photos?

Yes. iPad lessons can cover FaceTime, Messages, email, photos, apps, Safari, settings, passwords, and any everyday task you want to feel more confident using.

Do I need to bring my iPad somewhere?

No. Macbryte offers one-on-one coaching in the Ottawa area and can help online when appropriate. Learning on your own device is usually best.

Can you make the iPad easier to see and hear?

Yes. We can adjust text size, brightness, volume, notifications, accessibility settings, and other options so the iPad feels more comfortable.
