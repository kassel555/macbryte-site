Frequently Asked Questions

How long are the sessions?

Most coaching sessions last 60-90 minutes, depending on your preference and learning pace.

Do I need my own computer?

Yes, you'll need your own device for the sessions. This way, you learn on the same device you'll be using every day.

How quickly will I see progress?

Most students report feeling more confident after just 2-3 sessions, with significant progress visible within a month of regular practice.
