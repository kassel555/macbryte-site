Email Made Easy for Seniors

Email is still one of the most useful parts of everyday technology, but it can feel stressful when messages pile up, attachments disappear, or suspicious emails look real. This course helps seniors and beginners use email calmly and confidently on their own computer, iPad, iPhone, or tablet.

Getting Started With Email

  • Understand the difference between an email address, an email account, a mail app, and a webmail site such as Gmail, Outlook, iCloud, or Yahoo.
  • Learn how to sign in safely, recognize your inbox, and understand common folders such as Sent, Drafts, Spam, Junk, Archive, and Trash.
  • Adjust helpful settings such as text size, notifications, signatures, contact names, and preview options so email feels easier to read.

Sending, Replying, and Attaching Files

  • Write a clear message, add recipients, use subject lines, reply, reply-all, forward, and understand when each option is appropriate.
  • Attach photos, PDFs, forms, invoices, or documents without losing track of where the file is stored on your device.
  • Download attachments safely, save important files, print messages or attachments, and know when not to open something suspicious.

Organizing Your Inbox

  • Use folders, search, stars, flags, categories, or archive to keep important messages easy to find later.
  • Clean up newsletters, old messages, duplicate alerts, and unnecessary notifications without accidentally deleting important mail.
  • Create a simple routine for checking email so it does not feel overwhelming or endless.

Avoiding Spam and Scam Emails

  • Spot warning signs such as urgent language, strange sender addresses, fake invoices, refund claims, password reset links, and unexpected attachments.
  • Learn the safer habit of going directly to the official website instead of clicking links in messages that make you feel rushed.
  • Understand spam, junk filtering, blocking senders, reporting phishing, and when to ask a trusted person before responding.

Email on Phone, Tablet, and Computer

  • Understand why the same email may appear on multiple devices and what sync means in plain language.
  • Practise sending a message from one device and finding it later on another device, such as a Mac, Windows PC, iPhone, or iPad.
  • Review common problems such as missing mail, repeated password prompts, full storage, confusing notifications, and messages going to spam.

What You Should Feel More Confident Doing

Send, reply to, forward, and organize email with more confidence.

Attach and save files without getting lost in folders or downloads.

Recognize suspicious email patterns before clicking or sharing information.

Feel calmer checking email on your computer, phone, or tablet.
