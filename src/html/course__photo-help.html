Photo Help for Seniors

Digital photos are precious, but they can become scattered across phones, computers, iCloud, Google Photos, email attachments, memory cards, and old drives. This course helps you build a simple photo system so family memories are easier to find, enjoy, back up, and share.

Finding Where Your Photos Are Stored

  • Understand where photos may live: iPhone, iPad, Mac Photos, Windows folders, iCloud Photos, Google Photos, downloads, email, USB drives, or camera cards.
  • Learn the difference between a photo library, a folder, an album, a shared album, and cloud storage in plain language.
  • Check whether photos are syncing, duplicated, missing, or only stored on one device before making changes.

Importing and Saving Photos Safely

  • Move photos from a phone, tablet, camera, USB drive, email, or old computer into a safer, easier-to-understand location.
  • Learn when to copy instead of move, when to leave originals alone, and why deleting too quickly can cause stress or data loss.
  • Create a simple folder or album structure for family, travel, holidays, documents, pets, recipes, or other personal categories.

Organizing and Finding Photos Again

  • Create albums, rename folders, use dates, search by person or place when available, and mark favourite photos for easy access.
  • Clean up duplicate or blurry photos carefully without losing the originals you care about.
  • Build a repeatable routine so new photos do not pile up in the same confusing way again.

Backing Up Important Memories

  • Understand the difference between iCloud, Google Photos, OneDrive, Time Machine, external drives, and printed copies.
  • Decide on a backup plan that fits your comfort level, budget, and devices, without making the setup more complicated than needed.
  • Check whether your most important photos are protected if a phone is lost, a computer fails, or an account becomes inaccessible.

Sharing Photos With Family

  • Send photos by email, text message, AirDrop, WhatsApp, shared album links, or cloud links while understanding what the recipient can see.
  • Learn basic edits such as cropping, rotating, favouriting, deleting, and making photos easier to view before sharing.
  • Review privacy settings before posting family photos on Facebook or sending shared links outside the family.

What You Should Feel More Confident Doing

Know where your photos are and how they are stored across devices.

Create albums or folders that make sense for your family memories.

Back up important photos so they are less likely to be lost.

Share photos with family more confidently and safely.
