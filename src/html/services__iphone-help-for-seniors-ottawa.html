Common iPhone Problems We Help With

Many people use their iPhone every day but still feel unsure when something changes: a password prompt appears, a message looks suspicious, a photo disappears, or the phone asks for an update. You do not need to figure it out alone.

We can help you understand phone calls, contacts, Messages, FaceTime, photos, the App Store, Apple ID, iCloud, Maps, accessibility settings, notifications, and privacy choices. We can also help you decide what to tap, what to ignore, and what to ask about before making changes.

iPhone Help Questions

Do you teach absolute beginners how to use an iPhone?

Yes. Macbryte helps seniors and beginners who may be using an iPhone for the first time or who feel unsure after an update. Lessons start with the basics and move at your pace.

Can you help with calls, texts, FaceTime, and photos?

Yes. iPhone lessons can cover phone calls, contacts, Messages, FaceTime, photos, email, Safari, Maps, apps, settings, and any everyday task you want to feel more comfortable using.

Can you help with Apple ID, iCloud, and passwords?

Yes. We can help you understand your Apple ID, iCloud, backups, password prompts, two-factor authentication, and what to do when the iPhone asks for a code or approval.

Can you make my iPhone easier to see, hear, and use?

Yes. We can adjust text size, brightness, volume, ringer settings, notifications, accessibility tools, and other options so the iPhone feels more comfortable.
