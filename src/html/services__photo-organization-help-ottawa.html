Common Photo Problems We Help With

Many people have photos in several places: an iPhone, an iPad, a Mac, an old Windows computer, iCloud, Google Photos, Facebook, email attachments, and external drives. It is easy to lose track of what is saved, what is backed up, and what is safe to delete.

We help you slow down and understand your current setup before making changes. Together, we can create simple albums, find duplicates, back up important memories, share photos with family, and make a routine you can keep using after the lesson.

Photo Organization Questions

Can you help with Apple Photos, iCloud Photos, or Google Photos?

Yes. Macbryte can help with Apple Photos on Mac, iPhone, and iPad, as well as Google Photos and common Windows photo folders. We explain where your photos are and how they are syncing.

Can you help me remove duplicate photos safely?

Yes. We can show you how to review duplicates carefully, keep the best copy, and avoid deleting something important. We move slowly and explain each step before changes are made.

Can you help me back up photos to an external drive?

Yes. We can help you understand external drives, cloud backup, iCloud, Google Photos, and simple backup routines so family memories are not only stored in one place.

Can you help me share photos with family?

Yes. We can teach you how to send photos by email or text, create shared albums, use Facebook safely, or make simple links for family members.
