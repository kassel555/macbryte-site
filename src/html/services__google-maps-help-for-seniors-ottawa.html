Common Google Maps Problems We Help With

Many people open Google Maps only when they are already in a hurry. That is when it feels hardest to understand the screen, the route choices, the blue dot, or the spoken instructions.

We practise before the stressful moment. Together, we can look up familiar Ottawa addresses, compare driving, walking, and transit routes, save your home address, check business hours, and make sure you know how to stop navigation when you are done.

Google Maps Help Questions

Can you teach Google Maps on an iPhone or iPad?

Yes. We can help you install or find Google Maps, search for places, start directions, use voice guidance, save favourite places, and understand location settings on iPhone or iPad.

Can you help me use Google Maps for OC Transpo or walking directions?

Yes. We can practise transit, walking, and driving directions, including how to compare routes, check times, and decide which option feels easiest and safest.

Can you help with location sharing?

Yes. We explain what location sharing means, who can see it, how long it lasts, and how to turn it off. You stay in control of what you share.

Do I need a Google account?

Not for every feature. Basic searching and directions usually work without signing in, but saved places and some personalized features may need a Google account. We can explain the tradeoffs clearly.

Want to Feel More Confident Getting Around?

Bring your phone, tablet, or laptop and a few places you want to find. We will practise Google Maps step by step until the screen feels familiar.
