Microsoft Office Help for Seniors

Microsoft Word, Excel, and PowerPoint are useful for letters, forms, lists, budgets, volunteer work, family projects, and simple presentations. This course helps seniors and beginners learn the Office tools they actually need, with patient practice on their own computer and real-life examples.

Creating and Formatting Documents in Word

  • Create, open, save, rename, and find Word documents without losing track of where they are stored.
  • Format text with headings, spacing, bullets, alignment, page breaks, fonts, bold, italics, underlines, and simple layouts.
  • Use spell check, grammar suggestions, undo, copy and paste, tables, pictures, links, headers, footers, page numbers, and print preview.

Working With Numbers in Excel

  • Understand cells, rows, columns, sheets, formulas, and the difference between typed numbers, text, and calculated values.
  • Create useful spreadsheets for budgets, medication lists, contact lists, memberships, donations, travel plans, or household records.
  • Practise SUM, AVERAGE, sorting, filtering, formatting currency and dates, resizing columns, freezing headers, and printing clean tables.

Making Simple PowerPoint Presentations

  • Create a presentation from a blank file or template and understand slides, layouts, title boxes, notes, and design themes.
  • Add text, photos, icons, simple charts, links, and speaker notes while keeping the slides easy to read.
  • Run a slideshow, move between slides, use presenter view when available, and export or share a presentation safely.

Files, PDFs, Printing, and Sharing

  • Understand where Office files are saved on your computer, OneDrive, iCloud, Downloads, Desktop, or Documents folder.
  • Save a file as a PDF, attach it to an email, print it, move it into a folder, or share it with a family member or organization.
  • Learn the difference between editing a document, viewing a PDF, and opening a file someone else sent you.

Office Confidence and Troubleshooting

  • Know what to do when a file opens read-only, looks different, cannot be found, will not print, or asks about OneDrive or signing in.
  • Practise safely responding to update prompts, autosave messages, compatibility warnings, and file-recovery notices.
  • Build a calm checklist for common Office problems so you can save your work and ask for help with the right details.

What You Should Feel More Confident Doing

Create and format everyday Word documents more confidently.

Build simple Excel lists, budgets, and tables that are easier to read.

Make basic PowerPoint slides for family, groups, or volunteer work.

Save, print, email, and find Office files without as much confusion.

Common Questions

Can you help with Word, Excel, and PowerPoint on my own computer?

Yes. Learning on your own Mac or Windows computer is best because your version, files, printer, and settings match what you use every day.

Do I need Microsoft 365?

Not always. We can help you understand whether you already have Office, whether a subscription is needed, or whether another tool is enough for your needs.

Can you help me make a real document or spreadsheet?

Yes. Practical projects are ideal. We can work on a letter, form, budget, list, presentation, or other document you actually need.
